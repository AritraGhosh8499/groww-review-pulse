# Groww Weekly Product Pulse — 2026-10-21

## Top themes

- **Customer support & withdrawals** — many users report poor help and delayed payouts. (225 reviews, 170 negative, rising)
- **Charges & brokerage** — complaints about hidden and high fees. (189 reviews, 117 negative, stable)
- **App glitches & order issues** — frequent crashes and failed order execution. (132 reviews, 94 negative, stable)

## What users say

- ★☆☆☆☆ "Very bad support either it is chat or call." — Customer support & withdrawals
- ★☆☆☆☆ "Hidden charges on cancelled/unexecuted orders eat up most of your F&O profit." — Charges & brokerage
- ★☆☆☆☆ "Your app gets stuck far too often at the time of trade execution, and even after setting a profit/loss limit, the order does not get executed automatically." — App glitches & order issues

## Key observation

Negative sentiment is concentrated in support, fees and app reliability, with the “Extra charges on top of brokerage (external + DP charges)” issue cited 40 times. The support‑withdrawal theme is rising (26 this week vs 16.58 avg), while the other two remain stable.

## Action ideas

1. Deploy a dedicated support squad and faster withdrawal processing.
2. Audit fee structures, eliminate hidden charges, and clearly disclose brokerage rates.
3. Prioritize app stability fixes and add real‑time order‑status monitoring.

Source: 1830 Google Play reviews, 2026-07-29 to 2026-10-20.

### Identified fee issue

Extra charges on top of brokerage (external + DP charges)

### Explanation bullets

- Brokerage: ₹20 or 0.1% per executed order, whichever is lower, with a minimum of ₹5. It applies to each executed order, so a buy and a sell are charged separately.
- DP charge: applied per sell transaction of delivery shares — ₹3.5 depository charge (₹3.25 for female account holders) plus ₹16.5 Groww charge (₹0 if the debit value is under ₹100), plus GST.
- Statutory charges (set by the government, exchanges and SEBI, not by Groww): STT 0.1% on delivery buy and sell, 0.025% on intraday sell; stamp duty 0.015% on delivery buy, 0.003% on intraday buy; NSE exchange transaction charge 0.00297%; SEBI turnover charge 0.0001%; NSE investor protection fund charge 0.0001%.
- GST: 18% GST is applied on brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charges and investor protection fund charges.
- Auto square-off: open intraday positions squared off by the system are charged ₹50 per position, plus GST.
- Where to check: Groww's brokerage calculator estimates the total charges for a trade, and the contract note lists the final charges actually deducted.
